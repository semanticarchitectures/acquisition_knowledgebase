## Overview
This document specifies operational and communications resilience doctrine for Air Force agile combat employment. Command and Control (C2) nodes operating in austere or non-USAF facilities must withstand electronic warfare and satellite signal loss.

## Key Mandates
- **Communications Resilience (Section 3):** All tactical communications systems **SHALL** provide automated fallback to local mesh networking within **3 seconds** of SATCOM degradation.
- **Precedence Weight:** Level 3 policy supersedes AFMC, PEO, and Program Office requirement specifications.
